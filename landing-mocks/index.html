Three directions for the front door. Same content in each, different framing. Pull up on phone for the real read.

Option A
Emotion-first
"What are you going through?" + 8 human-phrased emotion cards as the hero. Today's strip is below the fold.
Best when the user is hurting and needs to feel met. Highest emotional pull, lowest decision friction.
Option B
Story-first
Today's strip as a quiet hero with pull-quote, then "or pick what's heaviest" emotion grid below.
Best when the brand identity is "the place with the daily story." Repeat visitors feel rewarded immediately.
Option C
Two doors
Big quiet question, two side-by-side doors: "I'm going through something" vs "Show me today's story." Honest about the two intents.
Best when you want zero ambiguity. Higher friction (one extra click) but extremely clear positioning.
